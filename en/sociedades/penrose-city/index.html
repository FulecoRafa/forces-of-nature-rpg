MORE ABOUT

At once the brightest and the darkest place in the Local Group, Penrose City is the meeting point of different societies that sought a refuge to escape the problems that brought about the decline of Navajo.

Through ancient gravitational technologies, this society was able to open portals scattered across Navajo to form wormholes and travel across enormous distances. With this technique, they transported structures to a black hole in intergalactic space, where no one — not even possible alien life forms — could find them, and there they began extracting energy through the so-called Penrose process.

After years of development, a growing population of gravity users and practically inexhaustible energy, they raised the city of Penrose, which came to shelter all who sought asylum. The city also served as a transport terminal between different places on Navajo, using the ancient network of wormholes left behind by their ancestors.

Penrose City prided itself on preserving peace through strict rules. After all, one could hardly expect anyone to challenge a gravity user in their own backyard. No one would want to be hurled into a black hole — it was assumed to be a fate worse than death.

OTHER VALUES

04 · SCIENTIFIC RESPONSIBILITYKnowledge with caution, power with responsibility.

Mastery of gravitational technology demands rigor, documentation and review. Research and operations involving energy extracted from the black hole follow strict protocols to mitigate risk.

05 · CONTROL AND SAFETY OVER GRAVIMAGICGravity obeyed by machines, the mind protected by rules.

Although gravitational manipulation produces effects that, without safeguards, can cause mental instability, Penrose society wields so-called gravimagic through machines and containment systems. This value prioritizes the responsible use of gravitational magic: only trained operators, redundancy systems and institutional limits authorize practical applications.

06 · ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION OF ORGANIC GRAVIMAGICThe machine controls the power; the flesh does not.

This is of the utmost importance: the use of gravimagic by organic means (that is, without technological mediation) is strictly forbidden. Historical and clinical experience has shown that direct manipulation — without mechanical filters and protocols — causes psychic collapse and systemic risk to the community. Violations are dealt with the utmost severity, including revocation of credentials and institutional sanctions.

07 · PRESERVATION OF THE REFUGE AND DISCRETIONSecrecy that protects, secrecy that saves.

Keeping Penrose hidden and functional is a priority. Discretion, technological secrecy and careful management of the portals protect the community from outside interference.

08 · RESILIENCE AND PRESERVATION OF LIFEWe endure together, we save lives.

The city values collective survival and the ability to withstand crises — be they environmental, social or military — without sacrificing basic values of humanity and care for its own.
