SOC-02TERRESTRIAL · MONASTICNATURE: SOULNO TECHNOLOGY

Silence
Penascle

A reclusive society of monks who preserve the soul and earthly tranquility. They strive to reach full enlightenment and to withstand the mental strain that humanity has suffered ever since it learned to manipulate the basic forces of nature.

They reject technology and practice a “magic of one’s own body”. Unlike the rest of humanity, however, they seem not to succumb to madness: their sanity remains unshaken and their minds, sharp. They use the powers of the soul to feed themselves and to control their surroundings, relying as well on animals that help with household chores. They live by simple farming.

Every so often, groups of monks come down from the mountains to preach and recruit followers. They promise eternal life and fullness of the soul. Almost no one who leaves with them is ever seen again, and finding the monks’ dwelling is practically impossible: the path is arduous and perilous.

It must be quite a skill, being able to use magic without losing your mind. I wonder: what’s the true cost of it? Is it really worth it?

— Belisky, the Troubadour

MORE ABOUT · WHAT THE ORDER HIDES

In truth, the order survives to this day by luring followers: they lock them in dungeons and redirect onto these prisoners the madness that magic would otherwise inflict on the monks themselves. The stock of followers works as both a battery and a sacrifice, letting the monks keep their sanity and prolong their lives.
