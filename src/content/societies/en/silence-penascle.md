They reject technology and practice a "magic of one's own body". Unlike the rest of
humanity, however, they seem not to succumb to madness: their sanity remains
unshaken and their minds, sharp. They use the powers of the soul to feed themselves
and to control their surroundings, relying as well on animals that help with
household chores. They live by simple farming.

Every so often, groups of monks come down from the mountains to preach and recruit
followers. They promise eternal life and fullness of the soul. Almost no one who
leaves with them is ever seen again, and finding the monks' dwelling is practically
impossible: the path is arduous and perilous.

> It must be quite a skill, being able to use magic without losing your mind.
> I wonder: what's the true cost of it? Is it really worth it?
>
> — Belisky, the Troubadour
