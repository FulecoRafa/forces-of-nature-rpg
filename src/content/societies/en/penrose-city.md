At once the brightest and the darkest place in the **Local Group**, **Penrose City**
is the meeting point of different societies that sought a refuge to escape the
problems that brought about the decline of *Navajo*.

Through ancient gravitational technologies, this society was able to open portals
scattered across *Navajo* to form **wormholes** and travel across enormous
distances. With this technique, they transported structures to a **black hole** in
intergalactic space, where no one — not even possible alien life forms — could find
them, and there they began extracting energy through the so-called Penrose process.

After years of development, a growing population of gravity users and practically
inexhaustible energy, they raised the city of Penrose, which came to shelter all who
sought asylum. The city also served as a transport terminal between different places
on *Navajo*, using the ancient network of **wormholes** left behind by their
ancestors.

**Penrose City** prided itself on preserving peace through strict rules. After all,
one could hardly expect anyone to challenge a gravity user in their own backyard. No
one would want to be hurled into a black hole — it was assumed to be a fate worse
than death.
